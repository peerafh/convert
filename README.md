# Convert

Paste or type any amount with a unit and it converts straight away, with no unit picking:

- Baht → US dollars. Dollars, euros and any other money → baht.
- Inches → mm, cm or m depending on size. cm and mm → inches (plus feet and inches for heights).
- Feet and 5'9" → cm. Metres → feet and inches.
- Miles ↔ km, kg → lb, lb → kg, g ↔ oz, °C ↔ °F, litres and ml ↔ fl oz or gallons, km/h ↔ mph, m² ↔ sq ft.
- Understands `30usd`, `$30`, `1,500 บาท`, `3/4 inch`, `120 x 60 cm`, `20-30 kg` and Thai unit words.

Open it with text already filled in: `https://peerafh.github.io/convert/?q=30%20usd`

Works offline once opened. Exchange rates are saved for offline use and refreshed every few hours when online.

Rates by [Exchange Rate API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com).
